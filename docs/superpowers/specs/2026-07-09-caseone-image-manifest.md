# CASEONE — Image Generation Manifest (for Gemini)

48 case-design artworks: 4 per style × 12 styles.

## Global settings (apply to every image)

- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 portrait (these are mapped as full-bleed textures onto a 3D phone case)
- **Resolution:** highest available (≥1024 wide)
- **Format:** PNG or high-quality JPG
- **Naming:** `<style-slug>-<01..04>.png` (e.g. `cyberpunk-01.png`)
- **Delivery:** drop all files into one folder/zip; I'll place them under `assets/designs/` and upload to Wix Media during seeding

## Prompt template

Append to every prompt below:

> Full-bleed abstract artwork designed as a premium phone case print, edge-to-edge composition, no borders, high detail, luxurious finish. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no phone, no mockup — artwork only.
